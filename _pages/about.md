I am **Šimon Prokop**, a PhD researcher at Brno University of Technology and a visiting researcher at the University of Bristol. My work focuses on visual-inertial odometry and autonomous navigation for aerial robots in GNSS-denied environments.

I am currently exploring robust state estimation with **OpenVINS**, synchronized camera and IMU datasets, and vision-based tracking of moving objects.

## Research interests

- Visual-inertial odometry and SLAM
- Aerial robotics and autonomous navigation
- Computer vision and state estimation
- Adaptive control and continual learning
